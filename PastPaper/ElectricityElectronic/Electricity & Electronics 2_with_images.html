Question 11

(0625/43_Winter_2019_Q9) - Electricity, Electron And Electronics

Fig. 9.1 shows a circuit containing an LED and two resistors in parallel, each of resistance R.

The normal operating voltage of the LED is 2.1 V and the normal current is 0.19A.

(a) (i) The potential difference (p.d.) across the LED is measured with a voltmeter. On Fig. 9.1, draw the symbol for this voltmeter connected to the circuit. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

(ii) The current in the LED is measured with an ammeter. On Fig. 9.1, draw the symbol for this ammeter connected to the circuit. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

(b) Calculate the value of R when the LED is operating normally. [5]

Tap to reveal answer

The p.d. across the parallel resistors is VR = Vsupply - VLED = 3.7 V - 2.1 V = 1.6 V.

The total current flowing through the parallel combination is the same as the current through the LED, which is 0.19 A.

The combined resistance of the two parallel resistors is Rparallel = VR⁄Itotal = 1.6 V⁄0.19 A ≈ 8.42 Ω.

For two identical resistors in parallel, the combined resistance is Rparallel = R⁄2.

Therefore, R = 2 × Rparallel = 2 × 8.42 Ω ≈ 16.84 Ω.

So, R is approximately 17 Ω.

(c) An arrangement of logic gates A, B and C is shown in Fig. 9.1. The arrangement has two inputs, X and Y and two outputs P and Q.

Output P of logic gate B has logic state 1 (high).

(i) Determine the logic states of the two inputs of logic gate B. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

Gate B is a NOR gate. For a NOR gate to have an output of 1, both of its inputs must be 0.

upper input = 0, lower input = 0.

(ii) Determine and explain the logic state of output Q. [3]

Tap to reveal answer

State of Q = 0. Explanation: The lower input to gate B is Y, so Y must be 0. The upper input to B is the output of gate A (a NAND gate). For gate A's output to be 0, both its inputs must be 1. One input to A is X, so X must be 1. The other input to A is also X, which is consistent. Gate C is a NOR gate. Its inputs are X (1) and Y (0). With inputs of 1 and 0, a NOR gate gives an output Q of 0.

Tap to reveal answer

(iii) With the circuit completed, the current in the 2.0Ω resistor is 2.5A. Calculate the current in the 6.0Ω resistor. [4]

Tap to reveal answer

The p.d. across the 2.0Ω resistor is V = I × R = 2.5 A × 2.0 Ω = 5.0 V. Since the 4.0Ω resistor is in parallel, the p.d. across it is also 5.0 V. The current in the 4.0Ω resistor is I = V⁄R = 5.0 V⁄4.0 Ω = 1.25 A.

The total current leaving the parallel combination is the current in the 6.0Ω resistor: 2.5 A + 1.25 A = 3.75 A (or 3.8 A).

Question 16

(0625/43_Winter_2020_Q10) - Electron And Electronics

Fig. 10.1 shows a relay.

(a) The switch in circuit A is closed. Describe how this operates the motor in circuit B. [3]

Tap to reveal answer

When the switch in circuit A is closed, current flows through the coil, turning the soft-iron core into a magnet. This electromagnet attracts the iron armature. The armature pivots and pushes the contacts together, completing circuit B and operating the motor.

(b) The switch in circuit A is opened. The soft-iron armature is replaced with a steel armature. The switch in circuit A is closed. Explain what happens when the switch in circuit A is then opened. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

When the switch is opened, the current in the coil stops, but because the core is now steel, it remains magnetised (it becomes a permanent magnet). Therefore, it continues to hold the armature, and the motor in circuit B does not stop.
