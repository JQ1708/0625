Question 9 (0625/43_Winter_2023_Q10) - Space Physics

(a) (i) 1. State what is represented in space physics by the symbol H₀. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

H₀ represents the Hubble constant.

2. Write down the equation that defines H₀ in terms of the speed that a far galaxy is moving away from the Earth and its distance from the Earth.

Tap to reveal answer

H₀ = v⁄d

(ii) The numerical value of H₀ is 2.2 x 10⁻¹⁸. State the unit of H₀. [1]

Tap to reveal answer

The unit is per second, which can be written as s⁻¹ or 1/s.

(iii) Use this value of H₀ to determine an estimate for the age of the Universe in seconds. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

1. The age of the Universe is estimated as the reciprocal of the Hubble constant: Age ≈ 1⁄H₀

2. Substitute the value:
Age = 1⁄(2.2 x 10⁻¹⁸ s⁻¹)

3. Calculate the age:
Age = 4.5 x 10¹⁷ s

(b) State when cosmic microwave background radiation (CMBR) was formed and where we detect it coming from. [2]

Tap to reveal answer

CMBR was formed shortly after the Big Bang and it is detected coming from all points in space.
